5. ONE-SCREEN BUSINESS VIEW

Dashboard utama harus mengikuti prinsip:

Prioritas informasi:

LEVEL 1 — WHAT IS HAPPENING?

Critical/High risks
Scan status
Evidence health
LEVEL 2 — WHY?

User dapat melihat penyebab perubahan:

findings
stale evidence
failed scans

LEVEL 3 — WHAT SHOULD I DO?

User dapat langsung menuju:

investigate
view evidence
start scan
remediate
rescan

Gunakan prinsip:

WHY → WHAT → DO

Jangan membuat user harus membuka 5 halaman hanya untuk memahami satu masalah.
